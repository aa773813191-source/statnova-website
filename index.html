About the Founder

I hold a Master's degree in Physics and am the founder of STATNOVA, a platform for statistical analysis and digital research solutions.

My academic background is in Physics, with scientific interests spanning medical physics, nanotechnology, statistical analysis, and research computing.

I combine academic knowledge with computational technologies to develop digital solutions that help students and researchers organize data, analyze it, and use it in scientific studies.
